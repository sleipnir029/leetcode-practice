description: Milestone assessment — a qualitative read on how your thinking is evolving
---

Write a dated entry into `ASSESSMENT.md`. This is the qualitative layer the dashboard cannot
compute — the numbers show *what*, this shows *why* and *whether the reasoning is maturing*.

Do this:

1. Read the current metrics: run `python3 build_dashboard.py` mentally or read `progress.json`
   for approach / recognized / mistakes / confidence trends.
2. Read the frozen `think-log.md` files and the `## Where I got stuck` sections of the `notes.md`
   files across `solutions/`. This is the actual evidence of thinking.
3. Judge, specifically and directly:
   - Are the pre-code plans getting crisper, or still vague?
   - Are complexity guesses landing before I correct them, or consistently off?
   - Is optimal-first improving, or stuck at brute-force-then-help?
   - Is the same class of mistake recurring? Name it.
   - Which patterns are genuinely internalized vs. merely passed once?
4. End with 1–2 concrete adjustments to the routine for the next stretch.

Keep it honest and short — a paragraph or two, not a report. Append, never overwrite; the value
is in reading old entries against new ones later.
